What Would You Like to Know? Frequently Asked Questions

What food and drinks does Aroma & Crust Cafe specialize in?

We specialize in authentic hand-stretched wood-fired pizzas made with 24-hour fermented sourdough, 100% grass-fed prime Angus smash burgers on toasted brioche buns, and freshly roasted specialty espresso and artisanal shakes.

Do you offer fast food delivery and takeaway?

Yes! We offer hot, contactless home delivery within a 10-mile radius in under 35 minutes, as well as rapid curb-side pickup. Every order is packed in heat-insulated eco-friendly packaging.

Are there vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-friendly options?

Absolutely. Our menu includes artisan vegetarian pizzas with roasted Mediterranean vegetables and four Italian cheeses, plant-based burger patties, and gluten-friendly pizza crust options upon request.

What are your operating hours and how can I order?

We are open 7 days a week from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM. You can order online by browsing our menu above, adding items to your cart, or calling our direct line at +1 (555) 276-6223.

Artisan Wood-Fired Pizza & Prime Burgers at Aroma & Crust Cafe

Experience the perfect blend of rustic charm and culinary excellence at Aroma & Crust Cafe. We specialize in hand-tossed wood-fired pizzas and premium prime burgers, crafted with locally sourced ingredients to deliver an unforgettable dining experience for every guest.

Why Choose Our Wood-Fired Pizza?

Our signature wood-fired pizzas are baked in a traditional stone oven, achieving a perfectly charred crust with a soft, airy interior. Each pizza is topped with fresh, high-quality ingredients, including locally sourced vegetables, artisanal cheeses, and premium meats. The result is a harmonious balance of flavors that satisfies even the most discerning palates.

We take pride in using only the finest ingredients, ensuring that every bite is a testament to our commitment to quality. Whether you prefer a classic Margherita or a gourmet creation, our chefs are dedicated to crafting pizzas that are both visually stunning and deliciously satisfying.

Prime Burgers: A Culinary Masterpiece

Our prime burgers are made with 100% grass-fed beef, hand-pressed to perfection and grilled to your preferred doneness. Each burger is served on a toasted brioche bun, topped with a selection of fresh vegetables, artisanal cheeses, and our signature house-made sauces.

From the classic cheeseburger to the gourmet truffle burger, our menu offers a variety of options to suit every taste. We believe that a great burger is more than just a meal; it is an experience that brings people together and creates lasting memories.

A Welcoming Atmosphere for Every Occasion

At Aroma & Crust Cafe, we strive to create a warm and inviting atmosphere where guests can relax and enjoy their meals. Our cozy interior, combined with outdoor seating, makes it the perfect spot for casual dinners, family gatherings, or special celebrations.

Our friendly staff is always ready to assist you with your order and provide recommendations based on your preferences. Whether you are a regular or a first-time visitor, we are committed to ensuring that your dining experience is exceptional from start to finish.

Visit Us Today

Discover the unique flavors and exceptional quality that set Aroma & Crust Cafe apart. Visit us today to sample our artisan wood-fired pizzas and prime burgers, and experience the difference that fresh, locally sourced ingredients make. We look forward to welcoming you to our table.

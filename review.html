Culinary Standards & Food Safety References

At Aroma & Crust Cafe, we prioritize transparency in our artisan wood-fired pizza and prime burger preparation. Our menu development and kitchen protocols are grounded in rigorous food safety standards and culinary science research. Below are the authoritative sources that guide our commitment to quality, safety, and authentic flavor profiles for our guests.

  • National Food Safety Guidelines: We adhere to the strict hygiene and handling protocols established by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) Retail Food Protection.
  • Academic Culinary Science: Our wood-fired techniques are informed by peer-reviewed research on thermal dynamics and dough fermentation from The Culinary Institute of America (CIA).
  • Meat Quality & Sourcing Standards: Prime burger ingredients are selected based on quality benchmarks outlined by the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) Food Safety guidelines.

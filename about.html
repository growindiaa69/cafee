The Secret Behind Our Artisan Kitchen

Founded on a passion for culinary honesty, Aroma & Crust Cafe brings together old-world Italian dough craft and modern American smash-burger grilling under one roof.

Every pizza crust undergoes a 24 to 48-hour cold fermentation process, allowing natural enzymes to build an airy, light, and digestible sourdough structure that bakes to blistered perfection inside 850°F stone deck ovens.

Our smash burgers start with 100% prime grass-fed Angus chuck, seared hard on seasoned planchas to lock in rich caramelized umami flavor, topped with melted Wisconsin cheddar and tucked into artisanal brioche buns.

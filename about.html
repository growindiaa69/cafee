The Secret Behind Our Artisan Kitchen

Cafee began with a passion for culinary honesty, blending old‑world Italian dough craft with modern American smash‑burger grilling. Here you get both traditions in one bite, so you never have to choose between pizza and a burger. Every dish shows that honest, blended spirit.

Cafee ferments each pizza crust for 24 to 48 hours in a cold environment, creating an airy, light, digestible sourdough base. We bake it in our 850°F stone‑deck ovens, so every slice hits your palate with a flavor you’ll love each time you bite.

Cafee’s smash burgers use 100% prime grass‑fed Angus chuck, seared hard to lock in rich caramelized umami. We melt Wisconsin cheddar over each patty and serve it in an artisanal brioche bun. One bite delivers that bold flavor you’ll crave again and again.

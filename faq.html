frequently asked questions

What food and drinks does Aroma & Crust Cafe specialize in?

Welcome to Cafee! We're in San Francisco, founded in 2021 by test. You'll find amazing hand-stretched wood-fired pizzas, grass-fed Angus smash burgers, and fresh espresso here. We give you fresh items you'll love!

How is your artisan wood-fired pizza dough prepared?

We cold-ferment our dough for 24-48 hours using authentic Italian flour. This slow process gives you an airy, easy-to-digest crust with signature leopard spots. We bake it at 850°F in our stone ovens.

What makes your prime Angus smash burgers so juicy?

We use 100% prime grass-fed Angus beef, ground fresh daily. When we smash it on our hot griddles, it locks in natural juices. You get an incredibly flavorful, crispy, caramelized crust every time.

Do you offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-friendly options?

Yes, you'll find delicious vegetarian wood-fired pizzas with roasted veggies, plant-based burger patties, and vegan cheese. We even have gluten-friendly crusts, prepared separately for you upon request.

What is your delivery radius and estimated delivery time?

Cafee offers hot, contactless home delivery within a 10-mile radius. Most orders arrive in just 25-35 minutes. We pack your food in thermal, eco-friendly packaging, so you'll get it steaming hot every time.

frequently asked questions

What food and drinks does Aroma & Crust Cafe specialize in?

We specialize in authentic hand-stretched wood-fired pizzas made with 24-hour slow-fermented sourdough, 100% grass-fed prime Angus smash burgers on toasted brioche buns, and freshly roasted specialty espresso and artisanal shakes.

How is your artisan wood-fired pizza dough prepared?

Our dough is cold-fermented for 24 to 48 hours using authentic Italian Tipo 00 flour, filtered water, extra virgin olive oil, and natural sourdough culture. This slow fermentation produces an airy, easily digestible crust with signature leopard-spot charring baked at 850°F in stone deck ovens.

What makes your prime Angus smash burgers so juicy?

We use 100% prime grass-fed Angus beef ground fresh daily. When smashed on custom steel griddles, the high heat triggers the Maillard reaction, locking in natural beef juices while forming an intensely flavorful, crispy caramelized crust.

Do you offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-friendly options?

Yes! We have dedicated vegetarian wood-fired pizzas with roasted Mediterranean vegetables, plant-based burger patties, vegan cheese options, and gluten-friendly crust alternatives prepared with separate cookware upon request.

What is your delivery radius and estimated delivery time?

We offer hot, contactless home delivery within a 10-mile radius. Most orders arrive within 25 to 35 minutes, packed inside thermal heat-insulated eco-friendly packaging so your food arrives steaming hot.

What are your opening hours and location?

We are open 7 days a week from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM for dine-in, takeaway, and delivery at 452 Artisan Boulevard, Suite 100, New York, NY.

Can I reserve a table or order bulk party catering?

Yes! You can reserve a table or order party slider combos and pizza boxes for family gatherings and corporate events. Visit our Contact Page or call our team directly at +1 (555) 276-6223.
